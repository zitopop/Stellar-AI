Settings guide

Know what every Settings area is for.

Settings should feel like a control room, not a confusing menu. This guide explains the main areas in plain English so users know what to press and why.

Open Settings in the appCompare plansGet support
Account
Who you are

Your sign-in, email and saved workspace identity. This is where users understand which account they are using.

Usage
Usage meter

Shows the remaining allowance for the current plan and when it resets.

Plans and billing
Money bit

Where users compare Free, Starter, Plus and Pro, manage checkout, and understand why upgrading gives more capacity.

Models
Brain strength

Where users choose the AI level for the job. Stronger models are for deeper coding, planning and business work.

Voice
Talk to AI

Controls microphone, spoken replies and language. It should be simple: choose language, speak, stop, and continue.

Plugins
Connected apps

Tools and connected services. Users should connect only what they need and keep important actions reviewable.

Privacy
Safety

Where users understand data, controls and account safety. This needs clear wording because it builds trust.

Support
Help

Where users go when something breaks, billing is confusing, usage looks wrong, or they need contact details.

The simple rule.

If a user opens Settings, they should instantly understand three things: what they have, what they can change, and what costs money.
