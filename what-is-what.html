What’s what

Stellar, explained simply.

You do not need to learn a control panel. Start in chat. Use the other parts only when the job needs them.

Main workspace

Chat

Ask questions, write, plan, code, debug, work with files and use web search from one conversation.

AI choice

Models

Fast is quickest, Core is the balanced default, Deep is for harder work, and Max is for the most demanding public plan.

Allowance

Usage

The percentage in the app shows how much of your current allowance remains and when it resets.

Extra capability

Apps & tools

Built-in tools work immediately. Connected apps need your approval before Stellar can use them.

Premium assistant

Jarvis

Jarvis is the premium voice, vision and assistant workspace. Plus includes Voice + Vision + Computer entry to StellarX computer control beta; Pro adds briefings and proactive alerts.

Work agent

StellarX

Use StellarX when approved work needs a paired computer, project files or structured actions. Higher-impact actions stay reviewable.

Rules in normal words

The TOS is the Terms of Service. It covers using Jarvis, connecting a PC or plugin, plans, billing, cancellations, safety and support.

AI outputReview important answers and test generated code before production use.
