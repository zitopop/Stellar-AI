3. Usage in normal words

Each plan has a usage allowance. The app shows a simple percentage remaining and when it resets. Heavier models, longer answers and more complex tasks can use the allowance faster.

FreeDaily usage that refreshes automatically.
Paid plansMonthly usage capacity that increases with the plan.
Usage barShows how much of the current allowance remains.
Reset timeShows when the current allowance refreshes.

5. What TOS means

TOS means Terms of Service. It is the rulebook for using Stellar, buying plans, using the service, using Jarvis, connecting a PC or plugin, approving computer actions, cancelling subscriptions, refunds, safety and support.

Do not write “no refunds” everywhere. It looks untrustworthy and can be wrong for UK customers. Use the Refund & Cancellation Policy wording instead.

6. Trust wording

AI output needs review. Do not promise perfect code, guaranteed sales, guaranteed rankings, unlimited automation or risk-free connected computer work.

Legal operator/controller details must be real. If the business details change, update the Terms, Privacy, Refund and Business Services pages.
