2. Credits in normal words

Stellar Credits are usage units. They pay for AI processing inside Stellar. They are not pounds, not a bank balance, and not something a user can withdraw.

Included creditsCredits that come with Free, Starter, Plus or Pro. These are used first.
Wallet creditsExtra top-up credits bought separately. These sit behind plan credits and are used when included credits run out.
Bonus creditsPromotional credits such as welcome/referral credits. They are for Stellar usage, not cash rewards unless a separate offer clearly says otherwise.
Model costStronger models can cost more credits per message because they use more processing.

4. Wallet top-ups

Wallet credits are for people who need extra usage without changing plan. Included plan credits are normally used first, then wallet credits are used automatically for eligible messages.

  • Wallet credits are not a cash account.
  • They should not be described as withdrawable money.
  • If a payment or credit allocation is wrong, users should contact support with account email and Stripe details.
  • Refund/cancellation rights still depend on the refund policy and applicable law.

5. Plugins, StellarX and computer actions

Connected tools are powerful, so the page should be honest: users must only connect accounts and computers they own or are authorised to control.

Good wording“Review the action before approving it. Keep backups. Disconnect tools when not needed.”
Avoid saying“Stellar can do anything automatically with no risk.” That is not safe or trustworthy.

7. Refunds and cancellation in normal words

Do not write “no refunds” everywhere. That can make the app look dodgy and may be wrong for UK consumers. Better wording is: refunds and cancellation are handled under the Refund & Cancellation Policy, Stripe records, the state of the service delivered, and any rights users have under law.

8. What makes the site look trustworthy

  • Use clear prices and say whether billing is monthly or yearly.
  • Keep support email visible.
  • Show the difference between included credits and wallet credits.
  • Do not promise sales, rankings, traffic, “millions”, or guaranteed business outcomes.
  • Say AI output needs review before it affects live systems, customers, code, money or accounts.
  • Make legal operator/controller details real before scaling paid orders.
