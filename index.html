Help
What Stellar AI does
Stellar AI generates working scripts for FiveM (QBCore) and Roblox servers. Describe what you need in plain English and Stellar writes complete, ready-to-use code.
How to use it
Type your request — for example a job system with pay, a shop NPC, or a vehicle spawner. Copy the generated file into your server's resources folder, add it to server.cfg, then restart your server.
Models
✨ Spark — fastest, for quick questions and small tasks. ⭐ Star — the standard model for everyday scripts. 🚀 Nova (Pro) — the most capable model, for large and complex systems.
Plans
Your message allowance refreshes every 6 hours. Lite and Pro make it 5× and 10× larger, Pro unlocks Nova, and paid plans can buy top-ups any time. Open Plans in the sidebar for details.
