Can I try Stellar before paying?+

Yes. Start free with £1 starting credit and 40 requests per hour. You do not need a card to begin. Wallet credit is separate from the included hourly allowance.

What can I build with it?+

Build or improve FiveM QBCore, ESX, ox_lib and standalone resources, or Roblox Luau systems. Describe your framework, dependencies and the result you want.

What do I get after a generation?+

A structured set of files with placement and setup guidance. Review the code, download the files and test them in your own server or Roblox place.

Can it fix my existing scripts?+

Yes. Paste the script and its error, describe what should happen, and ask Stellar for a diagnosis and revised files. Keep refining the project in the same conversation.

Do I still need to test the code?+

Yes. Review generated files and dependencies, then test them in a private environment before going live. Check server-side permissions, player state and rejected actions as well as the happy path.

Can I cancel my subscription?+

Yes. Paid users can use Manage billing in Settings to update payment details, view invoices or cancel. If billing management is unavailable, contact deadlyfox10@gmail.com. Cancellation stops the next renewal; paid access normally continues until the end of the current billing period.
