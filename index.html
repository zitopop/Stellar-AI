Questions

        
Answers before you join.

        
Find answers about features and billing. More help? Contact support.

      

      

        
Can I use Stellar without paying?
Yes. The Free plan includes up to 15 Stellar Fast generations per day without a payment card, subject to the published usage policy.

        
What can I use Stellar for?
Everyday questions, writing, plans, research, supported files and coding help. You can paste error logs or attach supported files. Review and test generated code before using it.

        
How do monthly and yearly plans work?
Monthly plans renew monthly; yearly plans are charged once per year. You can manage renewal in billing settings. Access usually continues until the current paid period ends after cancellation. Read the refund policy.

        
Does Stellar control my computer automatically?
No. StellarX requires a supported plan, a separately paired device and your approval for actions. You control the permissions.
