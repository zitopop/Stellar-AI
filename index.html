📜 Terms of Service

Welcome to Stellar AI! These terms are written in plain English. By using the app you agree to them. Stellar AI is an independent app powered by the Claude API from Anthropic.

Last updated: June 2026

1. What Stellar AI does

Stellar AI gives you AI chat, coding help, AI image generation, live web research, voice calls, and read-aloud. AI answers can be wrong — always double-check anything important, and test code before using it in real projects.

2. Plans & pricing

Free (£0): Unlimited messages until your usage bar fills — the bar refills every 8 hours. Includes chat history, images, and voice.

Pro (£10/month): Unlimited messages until your bar fills — refills every 4 hours (2× faster). Plus the Power model, all colour themes with a custom colour picker, AI personality, chat export, longer answers, and priority support.

Max (£30/month): Everything in Pro, plus the Ultra model (Claude Fable 5) with a fair-use limit of 75 Ultra messages per day, 2× longer answers, HD images, and the exclusive Nebula theme.

Wallet: Don't want a subscription? Top up from £1 and pay 10p per message (longer messages may use more).

Prices are in GBP. Payments are processed securely by Stripe — we never see or store your card details.

3. Cancelling & refunds

Cancel any subscription at any time — you keep access until the end of the period you paid for. If something's gone wrong, you can request a refund within 14 days of a payment and we'll review it fairly. Wallet top-ups that haven't been spent can be refunded within 14 days too.

4. Fair use

Messages are unlimited until your usage bar fills, and the bar refills automatically — every 8 hours on Free, 4 on Pro, 2 on Max. This is for normal human use, not bots, scripts, scraping, or reselling access. The Ultra model also has a daily limit (75 messages) because it costs significantly more to run. We may slow down or suspend accounts that abuse the service.

5. Your privacy & data

Your chats, profile and settings are stored only on your device (in your browser's local storage). We don't run user accounts or store your conversations on our servers.

When you send a message, it's passed to Anthropic's Claude API to generate the reply. Image prompts are passed to an image-generation service (Pollinations). Web research queries are passed to a search provider (Brave Search). These providers process your content to provide the feature — see their privacy policies for details.

Clearing your browser data deletes your chats and settings. We can't recover them — consider exporting important chats (Settings → Export chat).

6. Acceptable use

Don't use Stellar AI to break the law, harm others, generate hateful or abusive content, attempt to produce content sexualising minors, create malware, or violate someone's rights. Don't try to hack, overload, or reverse-engineer the service. We can suspend access for serious or repeated misuse.

7. AI-generated content

8. Voice features

Voice calls use your browser's built-in speech recognition and speech voices. Audio is processed by your browser/operating system (e.g. Google or Microsoft speech services) — we don't record or store your voice. Voice features aren't available in every browser (iPhone Safari doesn't support voice recognition).

9. Service availability
