FAQs

        
Good to know before you start.

        
Free access, plan details and how to get started.

      

      

        
Can I use Stellar without paying?
Yes. The Free plan includes up to 15 Stellar Fast generations per day without a payment card, subject to the published usage policy.

        
Can Stellar build and test code for me?
Stellar can help write, explain and troubleshoot code. You must still review and install the files in your own environment, check dependencies and test the result. Code is not guaranteed to work without testing.

                
What can I use Stellar for?
Use Stellar for everyday questions, writing, planning, research, supported files and coding help. Availability of connected tools depends on your plan and setup.

        
How do monthly and yearly plans work?
Monthly plans renew monthly; yearly plans are charged once per year. You can manage renewal in billing settings. Access usually continues until the current paid period ends after cancellation. Read the refund policy.
