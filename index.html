Can I try Stellar before paying?+

Yes. Start free with £1 starting wallet credit and 40 included requests per hour. No card is required to begin.

What can a business use Stellar for?+

Stellar can help structure customer enquiry workflows, plan automation, review websites, connect approved agents and assist with technical or software work when needed.

Does Stellar take actions automatically?+

Higher-impact actions should stay permissioned and reviewable. Connected agents and plugins are designed around explicit access and human control rather than hidden autonomous actions.

Do I need to connect tools to use Stellar?+

No. You can use the core AI workspace first. Connect plugins or approved agents only when they are useful for a specific workflow.

Can Stellar still help with coding and technical work?+

Yes. The same workspace can help plan, build, debug and review technical work, including software projects, when your business or team needs it.

Can I cancel my subscription?+

Yes. Paid users can use Manage billing in Settings to update payment details, view invoices or cancel. Cancellation stops the next renewal and paid access normally continues until the end of the current billing period.
