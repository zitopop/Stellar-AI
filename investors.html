The thesis

AI is most useful when it moves from answering a prompt to helping a person finish a real task.

Stellar AI is being built around that transition: a simple chat surface for everyday work, deeper workflows for larger jobs, and integrations that can connect the product to the tools people already use.

Business model

Start free. Pay when the product earns a larger role.

The commercial model is designed to let users experience the product before paying, then expand with recurring plans or incremental usage.

Revenue

Software first.

The core model combines recurring subscription revenue with optional usage purchases.

SubscriptionsStarter, Plus and Pro plans increase available usage and capabilities.
Add-on creditsCustomers can buy additional usage without immediately moving to a larger subscription.
Business productsBusiness-focused offerings can expand revenue beyond the core consumer workflow over time.
Operating focus

Prove the fundamentals.

The priority is not to make the roadmap look bigger. It is to make the underlying business stronger.

RetentionGive users a reason to return instead of relying on one-off curiosity.
ConversionMake paid plans understandable and valuable without artificial pressure.
DistributionBuild repeatable acquisition through useful content, search, referrals and partnerships.
ReliabilityKeep chat, accounts, credits, checkout and integrations dependable across devices.
