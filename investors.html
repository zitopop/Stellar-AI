Business model

Free entry, paid depth and usage.

Stellar uses a freemium model: people can start without a card, then upgrade when they need larger daily allowances, deeper models or more usage.

SubscriptionsStarter, Plus and Pro plans provide recurring revenue when customers choose higher usage and capabilities.
Add-on creditsCustomers can purchase additional usage without replacing their plan.
Business servicesSeparate business offerings can create additional revenue while the core software subscription grows.
