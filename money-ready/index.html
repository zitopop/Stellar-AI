What happens after payment?

Successful checkout

The user returns to Stellar with payment success state. Their plan/credits sync through the account system.

Cancelled checkout

The user returns to Stellar without being charged and can choose another plan or continue free.

Payment problem

Stripe shows card/payment errors securely. Users can retry, use another card, or contact support.
