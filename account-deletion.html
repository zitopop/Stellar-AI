Account and privacy

Request account deletion

Stellar AI users can ask us to close their account and erase associated personal data. This is the current supported request process; it is reviewed by support rather than completed automatically when you tap the link.

How to request deletion

  1. Send a request from the email address linked to your Stellar AI account if possible.
  2. Use the subject Delete my Stellar AI account. State clearly that you want your account and associated personal data deleted.
  3. We may ask for reasonable proof of account ownership before acting. Do not send a password, login code, full payment-card number or recovery key.

What you can ask us to erase

Requests can cover the Stellar account, stored chat history, uploaded content, settings, and connected-tool or paired-device records held by Stellar where applicable. Guest chats stored only on your device may need to be cleared in your browser or device settings. We will explain any information we cannot immediately remove.

What might need to be retained

Limited payment, tax, fraud-prevention, security or dispute records may need to be retained where law permits or requires. We will not treat deactivation as a substitute for a valid erasure request.

Subscriptions are separate

Deleting the account is not the same as cancelling future subscription payments. If you subscribed through Stripe on the website, cancel renewal in Billing settings or contact support. If a native store purchase is offered in future, use that store's subscription-management route. See Refunds & Cancellation.

Timescales and questions

We aim to respond to UK data rights requests without undue delay and normally within one month, subject to lawful extensions, identity checks and exceptions. This page is a way to submit a request, not a promise that deletion is immediate. For more information see the Privacy Policy.
