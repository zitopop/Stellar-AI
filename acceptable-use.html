Acceptable Use & AI Safety Policy

Rules for using Stellar AI, StellarX, plugins, connected computers and generated content safely and lawfully.

Last updated: 26 September 2026

1. Use Stellar lawfully

Do not use Stellar AI to commit, facilitate or conceal illegal activity, violate another person's rights, or access systems, accounts, files or data without authorisation.

2. Security and abuse

Do not attempt to hack, overload, scrape, disrupt, reverse-engineer or bypass Stellar's authentication, billing, safety controls, credit accounting, usage controls or rate limits. Do not use the service to steal credentials, session cookies, private keys, recovery codes or payment data.

3. Malware and harmful code

Do not use Stellar to create or deploy malware, credential theft, ransomware, destructive payloads or code intended to damage another person's devices, services, servers or data. Legitimate defensive, debugging and authorised development work must remain within systems you own or are authorised to test.

4. StellarX and connected computers

Only connect computers, repositories, stores, accounts, files or other systems you own or are authorised to control. Review plans and permissions before approving changes. Keep backups of important data. Higher-impact or destructive actions may be blocked or require approval.

5. Plugins and third-party accounts

Connecting a plugin gives Stellar only the permissions exposed by that connection and the feature you choose to use. You remain responsible for having authority to use the connected account and for reviewing actions before approval. Third-party service terms continue to apply.

6. AI output is not guaranteed

AI can be inaccurate, incomplete or produce code that does not work. Review, test and verify outputs before relying on them. Do not use Stellar as the sole decision-maker where an error could affect health, legal rights, finances, safety, employment, safeguarding or another high-impact area.

7. No impersonation or misleading claims

Do not use Stellar to impersonate real people deceptively, falsify reviews, fabricate evidence, misrepresent business results or make claims that are unlawful or misleading. Business automation should use verified information and clear escalation routes.

8. Fair access

A credit balance does not create a right to unlimited concurrency or to bypass technical, safety, fraud-prevention or provider limits. Automated traffic that degrades service for others may be restricted.

9. Enforcement

We may restrict, suspend or terminate access where reasonably necessary to protect users, the service, third parties or comply with law. Where appropriate, we may preserve relevant security records and cooperate with lawful requests.

10. Related terms

This policy forms part of the Terms of Service. It should also be read with the Privacy Policy and, for PC or plugin use, the connected-tool sections of the Terms.
