Priority summary

Focus first on the changes that make the business easier to trust, contact and buy from. Bigger redesign work can wait until the quick wins are fixed.

TrustMobileContactQuick wins
Finding 01The main call-to-action is unclear on mobile.

Why it matters: visitors may understand the service but still not know whether to call, book, request a quote or message. Priority fix: make one primary button visible near the top and repeat it after the service explanation.

Finding 02Trust signals are too far down the page.

Why it matters: customers decide quickly whether a small business looks reliable. Priority fix: move reviews, guarantees, service area, years trading or professional proof closer to the first screen.

Finding 03The next step after reading is not strong enough.

Why it matters: weak closing sections lose ready buyers. Priority fix: end the page with a short reason to act now, the best contact route, and what happens after the customer enquires.
