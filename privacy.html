Privacy Policy

This page explains what information Stellar AI uses, why it is used, which services may process it, and the choices available to you.

Last updated: 26 September 2026

1. Who this policy covers

This policy applies to the Stellar AI website, AI workspace, business-service pages, account features, billing flows, support interactions, optional connected tools and owner-only operational surfaces available through trystellarai.com.

For questions or privacy requests, contact deadlyfox10@gmail.com.

2. Information we may process

  • Account information: email address, display name, sign-in provider, session information and account identifiers needed to sign you in and keep your account secure.
  • Plan, Stellar Credits and billing status: plan name, included-credit usage, wallet/add-on credit balance, promotional or referral credit records, subscription status, top-up amount and bonus, checkout status, and Stripe customer/subscription identifiers. Stellar AI does not receive your full payment-card number from Stripe.
  • AI workspace content: prompts, chat messages, uploaded images and generated responses needed to provide the AI feature. Guest chats may remain in browser storage; signed-in chats may be stored so they can sync across visits.
  • Support and business-service information: messages, business details and service requirements you choose to provide when asking for help or purchasing a scoped business service.
  • Connected-tool information: where you deliberately connect an authorised tool or desktop companion, the service may process connection state, permissions, workspace metadata, selected files or task results needed to complete the action you requested.
  • Security and reliability data: coarse usage counters, request status, error events, device/session signals and security logs used to prevent abuse, diagnose failures and keep the service reliable.

3. Why we use information and our lawful bases

We use information to provide and secure accounts, generate requested AI output, sync chats, calculate and enforce Stellar Credit usage and internal safety limits, apply top-ups and bonuses, process billing, provide support, deliver purchased services, prevent abuse, diagnose errors and improve product reliability.

  • Contract: where processing is necessary to provide an account, AI response, paid plan, wallet top-up, connected feature or business service you request.
  • Legitimate interests: for proportionate security, fraud prevention, service reliability, abuse prevention and coarse first-party product measurement, where those interests are not overridden by your rights. You can object to optional product metrics from the Cookie & Local Storage Notice.
  • Legal obligation: where we must keep or provide information for tax, accounting, consumer protection, fraud prevention or other legal requirements.
  • Consent: where consent is the appropriate basis, for example certain optional marketing or future non-essential storage technologies. You can withdraw consent without affecting earlier lawful processing.

Essential account, billing, security and service messages are not treated as marketing. You can opt out of direct marketing at any time.

4. AI processing, recipients and service providers

Prompts, uploaded content and relevant conversation context may be sent to AI processing providers when required to produce a response. Stripe processes payments. Vercel provides hosting. Authentication, email delivery, account storage and connected integrations may also be provided by third-party services where needed for the feature you choose to use.

Recipients can therefore include AI model providers, payment processors, hosting and infrastructure providers, authentication providers, communication providers, fraud and security providers, and a connected service you deliberately authorise. We aim to send only the information needed for the relevant function.

Some providers may process personal data outside the UK. Where UK data-protection law requires a transfer safeguard, we rely on an applicable adequacy regulation or other lawful transfer mechanism and contractual safeguards. You can ask for more information about the safeguard relevant to your data by contacting privacy support.

5. Payments

Stripe processes checkout and payment-card data. Stellar AI receives payment and subscription status, the selected top-up amount, applicable bonus-credit metadata and related account identifiers rather than your full card number. Billing self-service may redirect you to Stripe's hosted billing portal.

6. Cookies, browser storage and sessions

Stellar AI uses browser storage and session data where needed for sign-in, chat continuity, preferences, referral/checkout state and other product functionality. Some guest chat state may remain only on the device until it is cleared. See the Cookie & Local Storage Notice for details and browser controls.

7. Measurement and error monitoring

We may count coarse events such as opening the app, starting checkout, completing checkout or encountering a technical error. Reliability telemetry is designed not to include prompt text, chat contents, passwords, full payment details or uploaded-file contents.

8. Sharing and selling

We do not sell personal data. Information may be shared with service providers when necessary to operate a feature, comply with law, protect the service, process a payment or deliver a service you requested.

9. Retention

We use purpose-based retention rather than keeping every category forever. Account and signed-in chat data is kept while needed to provide the account and related features, subject to deletion and legal or security requirements. Guest browser data stays on the device until it is cleared or expires under the browser's controls. Billing and transaction records may be retained for the period required for accounting, tax, fraud prevention, chargebacks and legal claims. Support and security records are kept only as long as needed to resolve the issue, protect the service or meet legal obligations.

First-party product metrics are designed as coarse counters rather than per-user behavioural histories. Where temporary individual-level technical information is needed to aggregate or secure those metrics, it should not be retained longer than necessary for that purpose.

10. Security

We use authentication, access controls and server-side checks to protect account and owner-only functionality. No online service can guarantee perfect security, so do not put passwords, API keys, payment credentials, recovery codes or other secrets into AI prompts or public community spaces.

11. Your UK data-protection rights

Depending on the circumstances and lawful basis, UK data-protection rights may include being informed, access, correction, erasure, restriction, objection and data portability. The right to object to direct marketing is absolute.

Your right to object: you can object to processing based on legitimate interests. You can also turn off optional first-party product metrics from the Cookie & Local Storage Notice.

To make a privacy request, email deadlyfox10@gmail.com. You may also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office if you are unhappy with how a privacy concern is handled.

12. Where information comes from and what is required

Most information comes directly from you. We may also receive account identity information from a sign-in provider you choose, payment and subscription status from Stripe, and task data from a connected tool you deliberately authorise. Information needed to create an account, process a purchase, prevent fraud or deliver a requested feature may be contractually or practically necessary; other profile, plugin and marketing information is generally optional.

13. Automated decisions

Stellar uses automated systems to generate AI responses, calculate credits, detect abuse and route product actions. We do not use Stellar AI to make solely automated decisions about customers that produce legal or similarly significant effects on them. If that changes, this policy will be updated before the new use begins where required.

14. Children and restricted use

Do not provide personal information about children or other people unless you are authorised to do so and it is appropriate for the feature being used. Connected tools and generated content must be used lawfully and responsibly.

15. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the service, providers or legal requirements change. The date at the top shows the current version.

16. Terms

This Privacy Policy should be read with the Stellar AI Terms of Service.

For billing, account, privacy, security or legal matters, use the support contact above rather than relying on a public community channel.
