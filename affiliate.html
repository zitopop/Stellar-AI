CONTENT ENGINE

Six useful things to post.

Short proof-based demos are stronger than generic “best AI ever” claims.

1. Fix a real bug

Show the error, the prompt and the final fix.

“Here is the exact error and the relevant code. Find the cause and give me the smallest safe fix.”

2. Website teardown

Use a real page and show the highest-priority friction.

“Review this page for clarity, mobile usability, trust and conversion friction. Rank the fixes by impact.”

3. Build something small

Make a focused feature people can understand in one clip.

“Build the smallest working version first, explain the files, then give me a test checklist.”

4. Compare workflows

Show Spark for speed and stronger models for deeper work without claiming impossible benchmarks.

“Give me a fast draft, then do a deeper review and show what changed.”

5. Plan a business task

Turn a messy goal into steps, dependencies and review points.

“Turn this goal into a prioritized plan. Separate safe automation from actions needing human approval.”

6. Continue a real project

Show that the value is returning to the same work, not just one flashy prompt.

“Continue from this project context. Tell me what changed, what is blocked and the next safest action.”
SHARING RULES

Market hard. Keep it credible.

A strong creator system should increase trust rather than trade it for short-term clicks.

TRUTHShow what happened.

Do not claim a workflow was tested, deployed, profitable or successful unless you actually verified that outcome.

DISCLOSURESay when you benefit.

If you share a referral link, make it clear that eligible sign-ups can give you bonus Stellar Credits.

PRIVACYProtect real data.
