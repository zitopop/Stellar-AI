What does your monthly allowance buy?

These are maximum included message counts if you use only one model for the billing month. They are not separate pools of messages; using different models draws from the same allowance.

Free gives up to 15 Fast messages daily. Free resets at midnight UK time; paid included allowances reset each billing month. Hourly request ceilings also apply. Message counts are based on current per-model allowance costs, not promises about response quality, uptime, or availability. Your app shows remaining usage.

Questions about plans and billing

Can I use Free without a card?

Yes. Free starts without a card and includes a daily usage allowance.

Can I cancel a paid plan?

Yes. Manage or cancel auto-renewal from billing settings. Cancellation normally takes effect at the end of the paid period; check the refund policy for refunds.

What can I use Stellar for?

Stellar supports everyday AI chat, writing, planning, research, supported files and coding help. Specialist code tools include FiveM Lua and Roblox Luau. Connected tools depend on your plan and setup.

What changes when I move to Plus?

Plus adds Stellar Deep, a 7,500 Fast-equivalent monthly allowance, longer output limits and Jarvis Voice + Vision or StellarX beta where supported. Computer control requires your explicit approval and setup.

What happens when I reach my limit?

Your included allowance resets automatically when your next period starts. You can choose a higher plan or an optional add-on if offered in your account; hourly rate limits remain separate. You can always see your usage inside Stellar.
