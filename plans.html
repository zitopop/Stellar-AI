Questions

Know what you are paying for.

Can I use Stellar without paying?

Yes. Free includes 75 credits per day, resetting at midnight UK time, and does not require a payment card.

Why would I pay for Starter?

Starter gives 5,000 monthly credits, longer output and enough Star capacity for regular use.

Why is Plus the main plan?

Plus unlocks Comet and 15,000 monthly credits, making it the best fit for coding, builders and deeper project work.

Who is Pro for?

Pro is for high-volume users who need Nova, 50,000 monthly credits and the longest public output limit.

Do wallet credits expire every month?

No. Bought wallet credits are separate from included plan credits and remain on the account until used, subject to the applicable Stellar terms.

Can I cancel a subscription?

Yes. Paid users can manage or cancel renewal through billing settings. Cancellation normally stops the next renewal while access continues until the end of the paid billing period.

Start where it makes sense

You can begin on Free.

Upgrade only when the extra credits, longer outputs or stronger models are useful to you.
