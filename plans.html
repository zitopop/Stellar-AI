Questions

Can I use Free without a card?

Yes. Free starts without a card and includes a daily usage allowance.

Can I cancel a paid plan?

Paid users can manage renewal through billing settings. Access normally continues until the paid period ends.

What can I use Stellar for?

Stellar is a general AI chatbot for questions, writing, research, coding, planning and troubleshooting. Optional tools handle larger specialist tasks when you need them.

What changes when I move to Plus?

Plus adds Stellar Deep, a larger monthly allowance and Jarvis Computer → StellarX computer control beta for deeper coding and larger projects.
