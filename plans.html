Questions about plans and billing

Can I use Free without a card?

Yes. Free starts without a card and includes a daily usage allowance.

Can I cancel a paid plan?

Paid users can manage renewal through billing settings. Access normally continues until the paid period ends.

What can I use Stellar for?

Stellar focuses on FiveM Lua and Roblox Luau scripting, debugging and review. The same workspace also supports general AI chat, writing, questions and research. Optional tools vary by plan.

What changes when I move to Plus?

Plus adds Stellar Deep, more usage capacity, Jarvis Voice + Vision and access to StellarX computer control beta where supported. Computer control requires your approval and setup.
