Questions

Can I use Free without a card?

Yes. Free starts without a card and has 75 credits/day.

Can I cancel a paid plan?

Paid users can manage renewal through billing settings. Access normally continues until the paid period ends.

Do bought credits expire?

Bought wallet credits are separate from included plan credits and are used after included allowance where available.

What does paid Jarvis include?

Plus includes Jarvis Voice + Vision and the Jarvis Computer entry to StellarX PC control beta. Pro adds Jarvis Pro briefings and proactive alerts. Computer actions still use the permissions, approvals and Emergency Stop shown in StellarX.

Which plan should most builders try?

Plus is the main builder tier because it adds Comet, Jarvis Voice + Vision and a bigger monthly allowance.
